The dataset includes 2265 images.
R-1-R-2-R-3-R-4A-R-4B-R-5A are annotated in YOLOv11 format.

The following pre-processing was applied to each image:
* Auto-contrast via histogram equalization

The following augmentation was applied to create 3 versions of each source image:
* Random shear of between -15° to +15° horizontally and -15° to +15° vertically
* Random brigthness adjustment of between -38 and +38 percent
* Random exposure adjustment of between -16 and +16 percent
* Random Gaussian blur of between 0 and 8 pixels
* Salt and pepper noise was applied to 10 percent of pixels
